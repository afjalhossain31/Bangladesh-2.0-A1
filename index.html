Starlink internet news

Date: 05/01/2025 Time: 10:25 PM

Starlink’s high-speed satellite internet service has started operating in Bangladesh, opening a new chapter for nationwide digital connectivity. This service is designed to reach remote villages, riverine islands, and disaster-prone coastal belts where laying fiber or maintaining mobile towers is difficult and expensive. By using low-Earth-orbit satellites, Starlink can provide more stable latency and faster speeds than many legacy satellite links, helping students, entrepreneurs, and emergency responders access real-time online services. Over time, this expanded coverage is expected to support digital education, telemedicine, e-government services, and climate-resilient communication networks across Bangladesh.

Bangladesh Scales Up Rooftop Solar

Date: 05/01/2025 Time: 10:25 PM

Bangladesh is rapidly scaling up rooftop solar projects as part of a broader shift toward clean and reliable electricity. These systems are being installed on homes, factories, schools, and commercial buildings to generate power directly where it is consumed, which helps ease demand on the national grid and reduce losses during transmission. By replacing a portion of fossil fuel-based generation, rooftop solar cuts fuel imports, lowers electricity costs over the long term, and reduces air pollution in crowded urban areas. This transition also creates new green jobs in installation, maintenance, and local manufacturing, supporting a more resilient and climate-friendly energy future for communities across the country.
